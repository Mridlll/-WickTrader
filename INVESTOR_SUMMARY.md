## Edge Explained

The strategy identifies **4%+ upper wick rejections** on SOL 4H candles - a rare but highly predictive bearish reversal signal.

**Why it works:**
- Large upper wicks = institutional sell pressure at resistance
- 4% threshold filters noise (lower thresholds = 0% profitable)
- Fixed 15% TP captures mean reversion without greed
- Wick-based stop loss provides asymmetric risk/reward
